**0:00 — Intro**

Meet DeskGadget. Your old phone or tablet just got a serious upgrade. A sleek, liquid-glass command center for your Windows PC. Your entire setup, right at your fingertips. Live stats, instant controls, zero clutter.

**0:17 — System**

Your PC. Fully exposed. CPU, memory, storage, network, power and uptime — all brought to life in stunning live rings. Lock your PC, kill the screen, show your desktop or snap a screenshot. One tap. Done.

**0:31 — Media**

Your media. Your rules. See what's playing, enjoy the artwork, skip tracks, control playback, adjust volume and manage every audio output and microphone. Total audio control, right from your phone.

**0:43 — Shortcuts**

Forget digging through menus. Your favorite apps and games get their own space. Big, beautiful, ready-to-launch tiles. Tap once. They're running on your PC.

**0:52 — News**

Your news. Your interests. Your feed. Get the headlines that matter, explore quick briefs from the regions and topics you choose, then send any story straight to your PC. No interruptions. Just one tap.

**1:04 — Touchpad**

And yes, your phone becomes a trackpad. Move, tap, scroll, right-click, middle-click. Your phone is now your PC's wireless touch surface. No mouse? No problem.

**1:14 — Windows**

Too many windows? Take control. See every open window, spot its app instantly, and bring exactly what you need to the front. No Alt-Tab marathon required.

**1:23 — The dashboard**

And this is where it becomes yours. The dashboard on your PC. Choose which modules each phone sees, reorder them, pick a theme. Add a module someone else built, or write your own in a folder. No app update required.

Open source. One hundred percent free. Android and Windows today. iOS is in the pipeline. DeskGadget. Your PC. Your rules. One swipe away.
